Welcome to Chronolog!

Chronolog is a simple attendance system for classrooms. Set up your subjects and students once, scan each student’s QR code as they arrive, and Chronolog records who is present, late, absent or excused—then keeps every record in one place so you can look it up, print it or export it later.

Your subjects, students and attendance are saved in this browser, on this device.

How do you use Chronolog?

  1. 1Add your subject and its schedule in Attendance.
  2. 2Add your students by student number.
  3. 3Start the class, then scan QR codes in Scanner.

Getting around

Move the cursor over (or tap) the logo at the top to open the menu. Each icon is a tab, described below.

Scanner

Records attendance by scanning each student’s QR code.

  1. Choose how to scan: Camera or USB scanner.
  2. For the camera, pick one and press Start Camera. For a USB scanner, click the input field and scan.
  3. Hold the student’s QR code to the camera. The result box shows who was recorded, and Recent Scans keeps count of present, late and absent. Scan the same code again when a student leaves to record their time out.
  4. Press Stop when you’re done.

Attendance

Your class workspace: subjects, schedules, students and the attendance table.

  1. Press Add Subject and choose its class days and times.
  2. Press Add Student and enter a student number, or press Import CSV to load a whole roster from a .csv file. Edit or remove students any time.
  3. Press Start Class when the session begins. Students scanned after the 15-minute grace period are marked late, and anyone unscanned becomes absent when you End Class or the class ends. Scanning a student a second time records their time out; leaving within 30 minutes marks them absent.
  4. Change a status by hand when needed—excused entries need a short reason. Use Export to Excel or Print for a copy.

Logs

The saved history of every attendance record.

  1. Pick a Date to see that day’s records.
  2. Pick a Subject, or leave it on All subjects.
  3. Read each student’s status, time in, time out and remarks in the table below.

    About Us

    Pardon the mephistophelian name—we are Sinister Six! We did not create Chronolog with the intention of becoming just another attendance management website among countless others, nor did we seek to compete with established platforms—for we ourselves recognize that such an ambition would be rather unrealistic and nugatory. Instead, our goal was to bring a project conceived, developed, and brought to life by students of Gordon College, for our very own academic community—similar to GC-LAMP. More importantly, we envisioned Chronolog as a means of helping both educators and students monitor and manage punctuality with greater efficiency and convenience.

    As first-year IT students, developing an entire website after only a few months of learning the fundamentals of programming was no small undertaking. Chronolog is far from perfect, and so are we. Yet, while we acknowledge its shortcomings and the limitations of our current capabilities, we recognize that neither defines the extent of what we can accomplish. We may still have much to learn, but we are certainly not beyond improvement—and this project is only the beginning.

    We hope our digital platform serves you well in your endeavors!
